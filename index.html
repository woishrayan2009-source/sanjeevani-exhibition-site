THE PROBLEM

The Golden Hour Problem

The "golden hour" is the window after a severe injury in which timely care most improves survival. In India, that window is routinely missed — not for lack of hospitals, but for lack of a system connecting patient, ambulance, and hospital in time.

THE SOLUTION

How Sanjeevani Works

Three steps, from the moment of the incident to the moment the hospital is ready.

STEP 01 🆘

SOS Raised

A bystander, victim, or connected sensor raises an SOS with live GPS location, instantly logged to the dispatch grid.

→
STEP 02 🧭

Smart Match & Route

The nearest available ambulance is matched by real-time distance and traffic, then routed to the patient along the fastest live path.

→
STEP 03 🏥

Hospital Pre-Alert

The nearest suitable hospital is notified in advance with patient status and ETA, so care teams are ready the moment the ambulance arrives.
